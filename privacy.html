Privacy policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Uhai Bora appointment and account application, why it is used, and choices available to people using it. Review it with the service operator and hosting provider before using the application with real patient information.

1. Who this policy covers

“Uhai Bora,” “we,” and “the application” refer to this website and its account, doctor directory, session, and appointment features. The person or organization operating a deployed copy is responsible for determining who controls the information and for meeting applicable privacy obligations. This policy does not identify a particular legal entity or clinic operator.

2. Information the application handles

Depending on your role and the features used, the application may handle:

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and password.
  • Patient profile details entered during registration, including address, national identification number, date of birth, and telephone number.
  • Doctor profile details, including name, email, telephone number, identification number, and specialty.
  • Appointment and session information, such as the patient and doctor associated with a booking, appointment date, session time, booking number, and status.
  • Technical session information required to keep account workflows signed in. The web server may also maintain ordinary operational logs according to its hosting configuration.

Please do not enter medical records or other sensitive information into fields that do not specifically request it.

3. How information is used

The application uses account and profile information to create and authenticate accounts, display role-appropriate pages, manage doctor and patient records, show available sessions, and create or manage appointments. The operator and hosting provider may process technical information to run, maintain, and troubleshoot the service.

This release does not include an analytics or advertising integration in its website code. A hosting operator may have separate infrastructure logging or monitoring; ask the operator about the configuration of the deployed service.

4. Where information is processed

Account and appointment records are stored in the MySQL or MariaDB database configured for the deployment. The organization hosting or operating that deployment can access the systems it administers. The application cannot establish where that infrastructure is located or which additional providers the operator has configured.

Social share buttons are links to third-party services. Those services receive information when you choose to open or use them and are governed by their own privacy practices; no social feed or tracking widget is embedded in this page.

5. Retention and account choices

The deployment operator should keep personal information only for as long as needed for the service and applicable obligations, and establish a retention and backup deletion schedule. This application does not define a universal retention period for all deployments.

Patient account deletion is available through the application's account settings. Deleting an account may also remove associated patient and appointment records in that database. For access, correction, or deletion requests that cannot be handled through account settings, contact the clinic or project operator using contact details they have provided to you.

6. Security and health information

Protecting health and identity information requires secure application code, server configuration, access controls, backups, and operational processes. This repository alone does not verify that a particular deployment has those protections in place. Do not use a development or demonstration deployment with real patient information until the responsible operator has reviewed and secured the entire system.

7. Children and applicable rights

The operator must determine the age requirements and consent process that apply to its service. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may provide rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. Contact the service operator or clinic to exercise rights available under the law that applies to you.

8. Changes and questions

This policy may need revision when the service, operator, hosting, or applicable law changes. The operator should publish an updated policy and effective date when making material changes. For privacy questions or requests, use contact details supplied directly by the clinic or project operator; this release does not publish a dedicated privacy email address.

This page is a project-level privacy notice, not legal advice. The service operator should confirm the actual data flows, retention rules, hosting providers, security measures, and applicable legal requirements before deployment.
