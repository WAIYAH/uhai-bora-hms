Cookie notice

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Uhai Bora's account workflows use a server-side session so sign-in and account steps can work. This release does not add an analytics or advertising cookie banner because no such tracking integration is included in the website code.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of data that a website can store in your browser. They may be used to maintain a session or remember choices between page requests.

2. Essential session cookie

When you use account features, the PHP application uses a session cookie to associate your browser with server-side session state, such as your signed-in account or progress through account creation. It supports core service functionality and is not used by this project to deliver advertising.

The cookie's exact name, security attributes, and lifetime depend in part on the PHP and hosting configuration. The operator should review and securely configure those settings for the deployed site. Signing in and other session-dependent features may not work if you block session cookies.

3. Analytics, media, and sharing

The landing page's illustrations, fonts, and video loops are served from this application. The project code does not currently include an analytics or advertising tag. Social share links open third-party websites only when you choose to follow them; those websites may apply their own cookies and privacy practices.

Hosting infrastructure or a future deployment may introduce additional cookies or similar technologies. The operator should update this notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law before enabling them.

4. Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential session cookies can prevent login, registration, and other account functions from working correctly. Browser controls differ by browser; consult its help information for instructions.

5. Changes and questions

This notice should be reviewed if the website adds analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other cookie-setting services. For questions, contact the clinic or project operator using contact details provided directly to you.

This notice reflects the website code in this project and cannot describe extra services configured by a hosting operator. The operator should audit the live site and confirm its legal cookie and consent obligations before deployment.
