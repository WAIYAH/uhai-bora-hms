A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good to know.

Need to get started? Create an account to access doctors, sessions, and your bookings.

Create your account ↗
How do I book an appointment?

Create a patient account or log in, browse the doctors and available sessions, then select a session to make your booking.

Can I choose a doctor and appointment time?

You can browse listed doctors and their specialties, then review the sessions currently available in the app.

Where can I see or manage my bookings?

Log in to your patient account to view your bookings. The patient area also provides options to manage or reschedule appointments.

Can I use Uhai Bora for an emergency?

No. Uhai Bora is an appointment-booking tool, not an emergency service. If you may be experiencing an emergency, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.
