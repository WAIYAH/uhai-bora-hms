CARE SHOULD BE EASY TO REACH

Accessibility

Last reviewed: 23 October 2026

Uhai Bora aims to make its information and appointment workflows usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies. This statement describes the design intent; it is not a claim of independent certification or full conformance.

1. Our approach

We aim to use readable text, clear page structure, descriptive labels, keyboard-operable navigation, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences. The landing page provides a text and illustration fallback for its optional decorative videos.

2. Known limitations

Some older account and dashboard screens use legacy layouts and may not yet provide a consistent experience across screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom levels, or small screens. The website has not been independently audited against WCAG, and accessibility barriers may remain.

3. Feedback and assistance

If you encounter a barrier or need information in another format, contact the clinic or project operator through contact details provided directly to you. Include the page, the task you were trying to complete, and the assistive technology or browser involved if you are comfortable sharing that information. Do not include sensitive health details in an accessibility report.

4. Ongoing improvements

We intend to review accessibility as the site changes and address issues that prevent people from understanding information or completing essential tasks. Applicable legal accessibility requirements depend on the operator and where the service is offered.

This project-level statement should be updated after a formal accessibility audit and when the operator has established a monitored contact channel for accessibility requests.
